Land Use / Land Cover (LULC) Classification Color Codes
========================================================

This file defines the class IDs and RGB color representations used for
visualizing Land Use / Land Cover (LULC) datasets.

Color Format:
RGB = (Red, Green, Blue)
Range = 0–255

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Class Name                | Code | RGB Color        | Description
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hazelnut                  | 10   | (238, 128, 5)    | Hazelnut orchards and plantations
Forest                    | 20   | (128, 255, 0)    | Dense forest and wooded areas
Permanent Cropland        | 30   | (242, 166, 77)   | Long-term cultivated agricultural land
Grassland                 | 50   | (204, 242, 77)   | Natural or managed grass-covered land
Sparsely Vegetated        | 60   | (204, 255, 204)  | Areas with low vegetation density
Arable Land               | 70   | (255, 255, 168)  | Seasonal or annually cultivated land
Urban                     | 80   | (255, 0, 0)      | Built-up urban and residential areas
Road and Rail             | 90   | (204, 0, 0)      | Transportation infrastructure
Water Course              | 100  | (0, 204, 242)    | Rivers, streams, and canals
Water Bodies              | 110  | (128, 242, 230)  | Lakes, reservoirs, and ponds
Wetland                   | 120  | (166, 166, 255)  | Marshes, swamps, and wetland ecosystems
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Notes
-----
- Each class is assigned a unique integer identifier.
- RGB values are intended for map rendering and visualization.
- Colors are selected to provide visual distinction between land cover types.
